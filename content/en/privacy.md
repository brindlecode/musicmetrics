_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

MusicMetrics ("we") operates musicmetrics.net. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the site.

## Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, email addresses or other directly identifying information, unless you contact us by email.

Our hosting provider (Netlify) processes technical data such as IP address, browser type and requested pages in server logs for security and reliability.

## Cookies, local storage and your consent {#cookies}

On your first visit a banner asks for your choice. **Advertising never loads unless you choose "Accept all" or switch on "Advertising"** in the cookie settings. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with **"Cookie settings"** at the bottom of every page; when you withdraw advertising consent, the current page reloads without ads. Cookies that third parties have already set stay in your browser until they expire or you delete them in your browser settings.

- **Necessary (always on):** your theme, language-banner and privacy choices (stored as "mm-consent") are kept in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager, with Google Consent Mode. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics cookies are only used after you consent; elsewhere they are on by default and you can switch them off in the cookie settings. "Reject all" switches them off everywhere.
- **Advertising:** only with your consent; see the next section.

## Advertising

We show ads from **Adsterra**, a third-party advertising network, on some chart, country, artist, song and weekly pages. They are labelled "Advertisement", are kept apart from our charts and never influence rankings. Ads load only after you consent to advertising. When they load, Adsterra's scripts (currently served from the domain bauval.org) and the advertisers Adsterra works with may set cookies or use similar technologies and receive your IP address, browser and device information and the address of the page you are viewing, in order to deliver, cap, measure and protect ads against fraud. Adsterra explains its processing in the [Adsterra Privacy Policy](https://adsterra.com/privacy-policy/).

We do not use pop-ups, pop-unders, push notifications, forced redirects or adblock detection, and adult ads are switched off in our account. If you see an ad that seems misleading or harmful, please tell us via the contact page.

Under some US state laws, ad cookies may count as "sharing" personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money; to opt out of this kind of sharing, choose "Reject all" or switch off "Advertising" in the cookie settings.

Our Google AdSense account (publisher ID listed in our ads.txt file) is kept for verification only: Google ads are not shown at the moment and no Google ad code is loaded.

## Third-party content

Song pages may embed YouTube videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); the video only loads when you press play. Images are loaded from Apple, Deezer and YouTube servers.

## Affiliate links

Some outbound links (e.g. to Apple Music or Amazon) are affiliate links. If you buy or subscribe after clicking, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The retailer may set its own cookies.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because we hold almost no personal data, most requests concern third parties above; contact us and we will help.

## Children

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA).
