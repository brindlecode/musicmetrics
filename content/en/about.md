MusicMetrics answers one question, every six hours, for every country we can measure: **what is the world listening to right now?**

We collect public chart data from the biggest music platforms, keep a long-term history of every position, and turn it into charts that are easy to read on any screen — with the numbers that matter: movement, peak position, days on chart and, for YouTube, daily views.

## What you can find here

- **[Global 200](/charts/global/)** — our cross-platform ranking of the most popular songs in the world, combining every chart we track.
- **Country charts** — YouTube trending music, Apple Music Top 100 (songs and albums), Deezer Top 100 and more, for dozens of countries.
- **Artist pages** — every charting song, every country, best positions and where to listen.
- **Song pages** — a song's position in every chart, its YouTube views and daily views.
- **[Most viewed music videos](/charts/youtube/most-viewed/)** — YouTube's all-time records and the Billion Views Club.
- **[Weekly recaps](/weekly/)** — an archive of what topped the charts each week.

## Independence

MusicMetrics is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube, Google, Apple, Deezer, Spotify, Last.fm or any record label. All trademarks belong to their owners. We only use data that the platforms make publicly available, and we link back to the original source.

## How we make money

The site is free. It is funded by advertising and by affiliate links (for example to Apple Music or Amazon). Affiliate links never influence chart positions — the charts are calculated automatically from public data using the [published methodology](/methodology/).
