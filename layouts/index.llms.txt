## Methodology (short)
Each chart position earns points: platform weight × market weight × ((N + 1 − rank) / N)^1.5, where N is the chart length. YouTube and Apple Music have weight 1.0, Deezer 0.6, Last.fm 0.5; the largest markets (US 3.0; JP, GB, DE 2.5; FR, KR, BR, CA, AU 2.0 …) weigh more. Points are summed per song across all charts. Movement arrows compare with the previous day; "days" counts days on the chart since tracking began.
